# Changelog

Newest first. One entry per session. Say what changed and anything the next session should know.

## 2026-09-08 — Base grounds

- Godot 4.7 project created. Third-person Smite-style controller, cel shader with ink outlines, procedural arena, training dummies.
- Form system: `FormData` + `AbilityData`, four starter forms (Human, Vantablade, Bulwark, Voltrix), 10 ability kinds in `AbilityRunner`.
- Mutrix: 15s combat lock, health percentage preserved on mutation, Overcharge bypass.
- Peer-to-peer multiplayer with ENet, join codes encoding IP + port, kill feed, scoreboard.
- Placeholder creature meshes generated by `tools/blender/gen_placeholders.py` (Blender 5.1) into `assets/models/*.glb`.
- Human concept art in `docs/concepts/human_turnaround.jpg`. Placeholder human matches its colors.
- Tests (`tests/run_tests.gd`) and `tools/check.ps1` (import, tests, headless smoke run).
- Known gaps: no animations, effects are primitive meshes, cooldowns reset on mutation, internet play needs port forwarding.
